2 PLAYERS MODE: SUMMARY (branch two-player-splitscreen)
=========================================================

STATUS (2026-11-07)
- 2 PLAYERS works on a real Wii (owner's log 1: "amazing it works").
- Split screen in races, side by side. Both views run at 30 a second.
- With FRAMESKIP AUTO the race holds 59.5-60 emulated fps.

LAUNCHER OPTIONS (PLAYERS page, last page; one press back from GENERAL)
- 2 PLAYERS: OFF / SIDE BY SIDE.
- GC PAD LAYOUT: A/B PEDALS (default) / R/L PEDALS (OLD).
- P2 CAR: FULL PHYSICS (default) / ON RAILS (OLD).
- P2 SCENERY: AROUND EACH CAR (default) / AROUND P1 (OLD).

HOW IT WORKS
- Split screen: the game still draws one view a frame. The cars take turns
  being the one the in-car camera follows. The renderer squeezes each view
  into its car's half of the screen, and each view clears only its own half.
- Timing: a render shows the scene built one frame earlier, from the camera
  of the frame before. So a render belongs to the car of two STARTRENDERs
  back. Frameskip decides for a pair of views together.
- FULL PHYSICS: the game's player physics runs a second time each frame, for
  player 2, on a swapped copy of its state. Player 2's pad drives the analog
  inputs. The CPU car is then placed where the physics leaves it, so it can
  steer, spin, crash and drive on.
- ON RAILS (OLD): the CPU car stays a kinematic AI body. Player 2 sets its
  speed and nudges its heading. The AI's pull back to its line keeps it on
  the road.
- AROUND EACH CAR: the course draws 3 of its 9 areas around the road segment
  of each view's own car. Without this, player 2's view loses its scenery
  once the cars are about 800 m apart.
- Player 2's HUD shows its own speed and rev counter.

WII TEST RESULTS (2P log 1, day course, in-car, two runs)
- Every frame drawn: 56-60 fps for the first ~80 s, then 54-58. One stretch
  about 2 km apart ran at 50-54.
- FRAMESKIP AUTO: 59.5-60.1 fps the whole race, 44-60 renders shown a second.
- Player 2 drove up to 181 km/h, steered, and sat at low speed for ~50 s.
- Left and right views stayed equal in every 10 s window.
- Cost against a single-player day race (log 25): roughly 1 ms a frame, mostly
  in the SH-4 code. Only a like-for-like course would confirm this.
- When the cars are ~2 km apart the renderer builds GPU programs twice as
  often, and those frames take 6-7 ms longer. AUTO absorbs this.

RUNNER CHECKS (Windows)
- GPU fetch check: 0 mismatches, 0 stale programs.
- Final combined run: 600 physics runs per 10 s; course placed around player 2
  for all its views; both views normal.
